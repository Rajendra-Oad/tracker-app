Privacy & terms

HTF is a local workout tracker. It does not create accounts or send your contact, profile, or workout data to the app server.

Before you start
  • Your profile and progress are saved in this browser on this device.
  • Email and mobile number are optional, are not used to sign in, and stay in this browser.
  • Clearing browser data or switching browsers/devices may remove or hide your saved information.
  • Exercise guidance is general information, not medical advice.
Data collected Storage and controls External services Terms

Data collected

HTF stores the information you enter so it can configure your exercise plan and track progress:

  • Optional contact details: email address and mobile number.
  • Profile: age, current and goal weight, fitness goal, and selected exercise level.
  • Training data: sessions, sets, reps, workout history, bodyweight records, push-up tests, and plan progress.

The app does not use contact details for sign-in, verification, or messaging.

Storage and controls

  • Profile and workout data are saved automatically in this browser's local storage. Use Save now on the Plan page to save manually.
  • Data is not synchronized to the server or another browser/device. Anyone who can use this browser profile may be able to view it.
  • Browser settings, private browsing, or clearing site data can remove saved information. Keep this in mind before changing devices or browsers.
  • Reset all data removes this tracker's saved profile and progress from the current browser.

External services

The adaptive coach runs in your browser and does not send workout data to an external AI service.

Opening a 3D exercise demo loads the bundled Three.js and GLB model assets from this app. These requests do not include your profile or workout history; a 2D fallback is available if they cannot load.

The hosting provider may receive ordinary technical information when your browser requests the app's static files. HTF does not send profile or training details in those requests.

Terms

  • Use HTF as a personal workout and progress tracker. The site host may maintain, change, or stop making the app available.
  • Training suggestions and progress calculations are estimates, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized professional advice, and they do not guarantee a result.
  • Choose exercises appropriate for you. Stop if an exercise causes pain and consult a qualified health professional when needed, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

This notice describes the current app behavior. The site host should review it against their deployment and applicable local requirements.
